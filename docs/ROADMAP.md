# Roadmap

## Objective

Finish VYOMTECH ERP as a production-ready enterprise platform that brings ERP core, lead management, and LMS capabilities into one multi-tenant operating system.

## Phase 0: stabilization

- finalize architecture and module boundaries
- standardize docs, naming, and folder grouping
- define environment templates and deployment standard
- secure auth, RBAC, and tenant isolation

## Phase 1: ERP core MVP

- tenant and company onboarding
- user, role, and permission system
- dashboard shell and executive overview
- admin and config workflows

## Phase 2: CRM and lead management

- leads and campaigns
- attribution and source tracking
- assignment and pipeline workflows
- reporting and automation hooks

## Phase 3: finance and operations

- invoicing and payments
- accounting journals and reconciliation
- purchasing and inventory workflows
- project and asset operational support

## Phase 4: HR and learning

- employee lifecycle and payroll
- onboarding and leave workflows
- LMS enrollments and progress tracking
- certificate generation and learning paths

## Phase 5: intelligence layer

- analytics dashboards
- AI recommendations and workflow triggers
- anomaly detection and forecasting
